MOUNT ROYAL

ABOUT THE PARK

Nature in the heart
of Montréal

Mount Royal Park is one of Montréal's most iconic natural spaces. Designed by renowned landscape architect Frederick Law Olmsted, the park has been a place for recreation, nature and discovery for generations.

From peaceful forest trails to spectacular viewpoints overlooking the city, Mount Royal offers something for every visitor.
